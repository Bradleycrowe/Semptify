Filing Packet Checklist
========================

Documents Required:
[ ] Completed complaint form
[ ] Lease agreement (copy)
[ ] Payment records / rent receipts
[ ] Communication log with landlord
[ ] Photos of property conditions (if applicable)
[ ] Witness statements (if applicable)
[ ] Notice to vacate / eviction notice (copy)
[ ] Proof of rent payments
[ ] Move-in/move-out inspection reports

Before Filing:
[ ] All forms signed and dated
[ ] All documents organized chronologically
[ ] Copies made for your records
[ ] Filing fee ready (check local court for amount)

At the Courthouse:
[ ] Bring photo ID
[ ] Bring all original documents
[ ] Arrive early to find parking/courtroom
[ ] Note case number assigned

After Filing:
[ ] Keep certified copies safe
[ ] Note all future court dates
[ ] Prepare for hearing
