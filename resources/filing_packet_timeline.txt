Filing Packet Timeline Template
================================

This template helps you organize your legal filing timeline.

Week 1: Gather Documents
- Collect all lease agreements
- Gather rent receipts and payment records
- Document all communication with landlord

Week 2: Review and Organize
- Review all documents for completeness
- Organize by date and category
- Note any missing information

Week 3: Prepare Filing
- Complete required forms
- Attach supporting documents
- Review for accuracy

Week 4: Submit Filing
- File with appropriate court
- Keep copies of all submissions
- Note filing confirmation numbers
