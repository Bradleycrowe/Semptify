MN Tenant Rights Resources

Security Deposits

Minnesota law requires landlords to return security deposits within 21 days of move-out, with itemized deductions.

MN Stat. § 504B.135

Repairs & Habitability

Tenants have the right to a habitable dwelling. Landlords must make necessary repairs within reasonable time.

Implied Warranty

Notice Requirements

Different notice periods apply for lease terminations, rent increases, and entry by landlord.

MN Stat. § 504B.161

Eviction Process

Minnesota evictions require proper legal process. Tenants have rights to respond and contest.

MN Stat. § 504B.371

Retaliation Protection

Landlords cannot retaliate against tenants for asserting their legal rights.

MN Stat. § 504B.285

Federal & State Law

Key MN Statutes

MN Stat. § 504B.135 - Security Deposits

Governs security deposit handling, interest requirements, and return timelines. Must return within 21 days with itemized deductions.

MN Stat. § 504B.161 - Landlord Entry

Requires reasonable notice (typically 24 hours) before landlord entry, except in emergencies.

MN Stat. § 504B.285 - Remedies for Landlord Violations

Provides remedies when landlords violate tenant rights, including retaliation protections.

MN Stat. § 504B.291 - Residential Tenant's Remedies

Tenant remedies for landlord violations including rent abatement, repair and deduct, and lease termination.

MN Stat. § 504B.371 - Eviction Action Procedures

Complete eviction procedure requirements including notice, filing, hearings, and appeals.
