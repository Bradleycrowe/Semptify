Getting Started

Semptify is your complete tenant rights protection platform. Here's how to use the main features:

📁 Document Vault

  • Upload lease agreements, notices, and evidence
  • All files are SHA-256 verified for authenticity
  • Download or delete documents anytime
  • Organize by category (Lease, Notice, Evidence, etc.)

💰 Payment Ledger

  • Record rent payments, deposits, and fees
  • Track payment history with dates and amounts
  • View total payments made
  • Add notes to each payment

📅 Timeline

  • Document your housing journey chronologically
  • Record issues, maintenance, payments, communications
  • Use categories to organize events
  • Build a comprehensive case history

⚖️ Legal Tools

  • Complaint Filing: Generate court-ready legal documents
  • Dakota Library: Access MN-specific housing laws
  • Jurisdiction Engine: Get location-specific guidance
  • Brad AI: Ask questions about your rights

📸 Evidence Capture

  • Upload photos of property issues
  • Record audio/video evidence
  • Automatic metadata and SHA-256 hashing
  • Timestamps for authenticity

🔒 Security & Privacy

All files are stored locally. SHA-256 hashing ensures document integrity. Your data stays on your device.

📞 Need More Help?

Contact your local tenant rights organization or legal aid for personalized assistance.
